
### Pitch Deck & PDF Submission Blueprint

When you step into your role as the Documentation and Pitch Lead, organize your deliverables using this exact structure to guarantee you address every metric the professors care about.

#### Google Slides Pitch Outline (Max 5-7 Minutes)

* **Slide 1**: The Sustainability Crisis — Define the real-world baseline of your lottery project (e.g., global logistics emissions or agricultural nitrogen footprints).
* **Slide 2**: The Classical Bottleneck — Explain exactly why classical supercomputers scale exponentially and fail to solve this specific problem.
* **Slide 3**: Our Quantum Mapping — Present your formulation strategy (How your team turned the problem into a QUBO, Hamiltonian, or Quantum Feature Map).
* **Slide 4**: Circuit Architecture — Show your circuit design (QAOA ansatz, VQE layers, or QNN structure).
* **Slide 5**: Results & Scalability — Display your optimization convergence graphs and explain your path to a true quantum advantage as hardware scales.

#### Markdown-to-PDF Technical Report Layout

Have your documentation lead write a clean, text-based markdown file in your GitHub repo called REPORT.md. When finished, use the built-in browser print tool (File > Print > Save as PDF) to generate a clean, submission-ready PDF. Use this structure:

**1. Abstract**: A brief 200-word overview of your sustainability solution.

**2. Mathematical Formulation**: The explicit algebraic equations of your constraints or molecular mappings.

**3. Quantum Implementation Details**: Specific parameters used (optimizers, shots, layers, backends).

**4. Discussion & References**: Cite the foundational papers and poster presentations from the main ICTP-SAIFR school database.
